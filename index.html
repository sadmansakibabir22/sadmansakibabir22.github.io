Questions owners ask

What if it gives a customer a wrong answer?

It only answers from the information you approve, and you check its answers before launch. When a question falls outside that, it says so and passes it to your team instead of guessing.

Do I need a new website?

No. The chat window is added to your existing website with one small piece of code, and I can do that for you. It can also work only on WhatsApp or Facebook if you don't have a website.

Can it reply in Arabic, Spanish or other languages?

Yes. It can answer in English and most major languages, including Arabic, Spanish, French and Hindi, and replies in the language the customer writes in.

My clinic gets medical questions. Is that safe?

The assistant shares general information like prices, hours and how to book. It doesn't give medical advice, and urgent or clinical questions are sent to your phone line or staff.
